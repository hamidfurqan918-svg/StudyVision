📖 Quick Notes

1. Electric Charge

Electric charge is a fundamental property of matter. There are two types of charge: positive and negative. Like charges repel each other while unlike charges attract.

2. Coulomb's Law

The electrostatic force between two point charges depends on the magnitude of the charges and the distance between them.

3. Electric Field

Electric field at a point is the force experienced by a unit positive test charge placed at that point.

4. Electric Field Lines

Electric field lines are imaginary lines used to represent the direction and strength of an electric field.

⭐ Important Questions

Q2. What is an electric field?

Electric field is the force experienced by a unit positive test charge at a point.
